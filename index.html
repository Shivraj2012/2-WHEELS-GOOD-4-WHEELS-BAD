BIKE ZONE

Superbike - Built For Speed

A superbike is a powerful motorcycle designed for high speed, fast acceleration, and sharp cornering. It has a strong engine, sporty tyres, powerful brakes, and advanced suspension. The engine sends power through the gearbox and chain to the rear wheel. Its aerodynamic design helps reduce air resistance. Superbikes are mainly used on roads and racing tracks and are best suited for experienced riders.

Dirt Bikes - Made for Off-Road Riding

A motocross(Dirt Bikes) bike is a lightweight motorcycle designed for dirt tracks, jumps, and rough terrain. It has special tyres that provide strong grip on mud, sand, and loose ground. Its long suspension helps absorb bumps and makes jumping safer. The engine sends power through the gearbox and chain to the rear wheel. These bikes are built to be strong, lightweight, and easy to control off-road.

Street Bikes - Perfect For The Road

A street bike is a motorcycle designed for everyday road riding, commuting, and travelling. It usually has comfortable seating, road tyres, good brakes, and suspension for a smoother ride. The engine produces power that travels through the gearbox and chain to the rear wheel. Street bikes are practical and easier to control at normal speeds, making them suitable for regular road use and enjoyable rides.

Mountain Bike – Built for Adventure

A mountain bike is a pedal-powered bicycle designed for hills, mountains, dirt trails, and rough surfaces. It has wide tyres for better grip, strong brakes, and suspension to absorb bumps. The rider creates movement by pushing the pedals, which turn the chain and rear wheel. Different gears make climbing hills easier. Its strong frame allows it to handle challenging trails and uneven ground.
